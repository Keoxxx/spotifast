SPOTIFAST SOLOIST PREVIEW

A test build, before release. Keep your usual Spotifast app installed.

Mac: quit Spotifast, then open Spotifast Preview. It uses your saved setup.
Allow System Audio Recording when macOS asks. Start a song through Soloist;
the equalizer, spectrum, oscilloscope and MilkDrop become available once
Spotifast receives its audio. Requires macOS 14.2 or newer.

Windows: open spotifast.exe in this folder. This portable copy keeps its own
settings in spotifast-data and copies your installed app's setup the first
time. Start a song through Soloist to enable the visualizers and MilkDrop.
Soloist EQ is not available on Windows. This preview executable is unsigned.

Mac: once capture is ready, Spotifast's slider changes its own output volume,
so visualizers keep moving even at zero. Spotify/phone volume is separate
and still affects the visuals. Before capture is ready, including briefly
after reconnecting, Soloist uses its ordinary output and source volume.

Windows: capture follows Soloist's volume, including mute.
Only Spotifast's own player is captured; no audio is saved.
A Mac output-device or format change currently requires reconnecting Soloist.
Use Settings to turn Soloist off and return to the built-in player.
